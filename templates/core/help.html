How to use Gather HQs

Step-by-step guidance for your account, organizations, events, communications, payments, and operations tools.

Account and profile

  • Open your profile from the top-right avatar menu to update your name, photo, and mailing address.
  • Use Organizations to open your organization list and manage all organizations where you have access.
  • Use Log out from the same profile menu to securely end your session.

Organizations and website

  • Organizations opens your organization list and account workspace.
  • Open workspace opens a specific organization workspace.
  • Visit site opens your public website for quick content and appearance checks.
  • Website settings and Quick start in each organization workspace guide launch readiness.

Events and attendance

  • Create single or recurring events, manage visibility, and send invitations.
  • Track registrations and check-ins using rosters and attendance views.
  • Use reports to monitor registrations, participation, and post-event trends.

Notifications and communications

  • Use the bell icon to review recent unread updates without leaving your current page.
  • Open Notifications for full history and mark items read individually or all at once.
  • Use campaigns and delivery status tools to manage newsletters and message follow-up.

Billing and payments

  • Subscriber admins can manage trial, subscription, and Stripe connection from the organization workspace.
  • Ticket fees and refunds are tracked in reporting and platform operations summaries.
  • For policy details, review legal pages linked in the footer.

Support and operations

  • Platform administrators can use Platform operations for support, moderation, and retention actions.
  • Sensitive operations are audited and require role-based access.
