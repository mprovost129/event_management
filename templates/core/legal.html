{% if document == 'privacy' %}

Privacy notice

Gather HQs provides websites, event registration, communications, attendance, reviews, and connected-account payment tools for independent groups. Group leaders control the contact and event information collected through their site; Gather HQs processes that information to operate the service.

Information handled

This can include account details, contact information, event responses, named guests, attendance, communication preferences, reviews, and transaction records. Payment-card details are collected by Stripe-hosted pages and are not stored by Gather HQs.

How information is used

Information is used to provide requested event and membership services, deliver operational messages, send marketing only under the recorded channel consent, prevent abuse, support users, and maintain security and financial history.

Choices and retention

Marketing messages include an unsubscribe path. Subscriber administrators can export their site data. Deletion requests follow a documented retention and approval process so they are not performed accidentally. Contact the group shown on a subscriber site for corrections to that group's records; platform account requests can be directed to the Gather HQs support contact published before launch.

{% elif document == 'terms' %}

Terms of service

Gather HQs is a scheduling and event-management service for adult-led groups. Subscribers are responsible for the accuracy of their event information, invitations, refund descriptions, member policies, content, and compliance obligations.

Payments

Platform subscriptions are charged by Gather HQs. Event tickets and group membership dues are processed for the subscriber through its connected Stripe account. Gather HQs currently takes no application fee from those transactions. Provider fees, refunds, and disputes remain subject to the connected account's arrangements with Stripe.

Service use

Users must protect their credentials, use attendee and contact information only for legitimate group operations, honor communication preferences, and follow the acceptable-use and review policies. Access may be suspended to protect users, the platform, or third parties.

Availability

The service may change as it develops. Final warranty, liability, governing-law, dispute, cancellation, and refund terms must be approved and inserted before public launch.

{% elif document == 'acceptable_use' %}

Acceptable-use policy

Do not use Gather HQs to break the law, mislead attendees, harass people, exploit minors, distribute malware, probe systems without authorization, evade consent or suppression, send unsolicited bulk messages, impersonate others, or collect payment for deceptive events.

Subscribers must have a legitimate basis for the contacts they enter and must honor email and SMS choices. Do not upload confidential payment credentials, government identifiers, medical records, or other information the product does not request.

Gather HQs may restrict or suspend access while investigating safety, security, fraud, or abuse concerns. A final enforcement and appeal contact must be published before launch.

{% else %}

Review guidelines

Reviews are available only to participants recorded as checked in after an event ends. Describe your own experience. Do not include threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, private contact details, payment credentials, or claims unrelated to the event.

Group staff may respond and may report a review, but cannot hide it merely because the rating is negative. Reported reviews remain public until a platform administrator evaluates the report. Platform administrators must record a reason when hiding or restoring content.

Reviewers can edit or remove their public review through their private review link. Moderation decisions may preserve limited audit history even when public content is removed.
